CHARLEY TUSLER

Vision Technician & Emerging Designer

I'm a Meanjin (Brisbane) based vision technician and emerging designer with hands-on experience across every technical field - vision, lighting, audio and backline. I've worked on everything from student theatre to large-scale festivals, both independently and as part of diverse crews, and I love being the person who makes the screens, cameras and visuals come together on the night.

I currently work as a vision and lighting technician with Twisted Pair Productions, an AV technician at Brisbane Powerhouse, and a vision and LED technician with IJS Productions. That work has taken me to Gympie Music Muster, Ultra Beach Gold Coast, Rolling Sets and Outback Sounds in Charleville, running LED screen systems, operating cameras and PTZs, and switching live vision.

Alongside that, I'm studying a Bachelor of Fine Arts in Technical Production at QUT and moving into design. In 2026 I designed vision systems and content for QUT Acting's Incognito and the Freshblood Festival play Winging It, and assisted the lighting designer at Dark Mofo while switching vision for Electron.

Ultimately, I want to create experiences that really connect with audiences - especially in theatre, where vision and lighting can shape the whole look and feel of a production. I'm always keen to collaborate with people who want to push what live events can be.
